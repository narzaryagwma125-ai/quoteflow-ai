# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-13_

This policy describes what QuoteFlow AI ("the Service", "we") collects, why, and how it's handled. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account data.** When you sign up we store your email address and a cryptographic hash of your password (never the plaintext password).

**Business and customer data.** Data you enter: your business profile (name, contact details, tax rate, logo), your customers' contact details, and the quotes, notes, and terms you create.

**Usage and billing data.** Monthly usage counters (quotes created, AI assists) and subscription status, including third-party payment records of the fact that you hold a subscription. We do not see or store credit card numbers.

**Technical logs.** Server and audit logs record actions, entity IDs, timestamps, and a hashed IP prefix. They never contain request bodies, passwords, tokens, or payment details.

**Public quote submissions.** When a customer accepts or rejects a quote link we store the name and optional email they provide.

## 2. How we use data

- To provide, operate, and secure the Service (quotes, sharing, PDF generation, reminders).
- To calculate plan usage and limits, and to bill via our payment provider.
- To send verification, password-reset, and transactional emails.
- To power AI-assisted writing when you request it (see §4).

## 3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with:

- **Payment processor (Razorpay)** — for subscription checkout and webhooks. It receives your email and a platform user ID.
- **AI provider (Google Gemini)** — only when you explicitly request an AI task; see §4.
- **Sub-processors for infrastructure/email** where strictly necessary to run the Service, each bound by confidentiality.

Public quote links intentionally expose appropriate quote details to anyone holding the link.

## 4. AI processing

AI features are opt-in per action. When you click an AI button, the relevant quote/customer context (e.g. service names, a quote number, customer name for a follow-up) is sent to the AI provider to generate text. Prompt text may be processed by external providers. Do not paste sensitive personal data you are not comfortable sharing. Prompt results may be shown for your review before use.

## 5. Data retention

- Account data is kept while your account is active.
- Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) permanently deletes your account, sessions, business profile, customers, quotes, payment-event references, and usage counters.
- Audit logs are retained for a limited period for security purposes.
- Despite deletion, our payment provider may retain billing records required by law.

## 6. Data subject rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. You can manage most data directly in the product; contact us with requests or questions.

## 7. Security

We apply the controls described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), including encryption of data in transit (TLS), hashed secrets, rate limiting, and server-side validation. No method of transmission is 100% secure; you use the Service at your own risk within our stated safeguards.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction) and is offered to businesses.

## 9. Cookies

Workspace: we rely primarily on an HttpOnly session cookie and a CSRF token cookie necessary for the Service to function. We do not use advertising cookies.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced through the Service or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 11. Contact

Use the contact details published on the product's site for privacy questions or requests.
